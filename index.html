🤖 JobTab

AI-powered platform for jobs, experience, and growth

Problem

Many people cannot find jobs because they lack experience. But without a job, they cannot gain experience.

Solution

JobTab is a Telegram-based platform that connects employers and workers instantly. Our AI matches tasks with users based on their skills.

How it works

  • Employer posts a task
  • AI categorizes it automatically
  • Worker receives relevant tasks
  • Applies in one click

Certificates

Users earn certificates after completing tasks. This helps them build real experience and a strong portfolio.

Why it matters

We help students, beginners, and unemployed people start their careers.
